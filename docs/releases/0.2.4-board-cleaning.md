# 0.2.4: clean today's holds

Status: implemented on `feat/0.2.4-board-cleaning`; since the owner's decision
of 2026-10-24 the holds come from the logbook (`feat/0.2.4-cleaning-logged`).
Pending owner integration and physical board validation. This is Android
feature work, not a published 0.2.4 release or a claim of iOS parity.

## Entry and behavior

Open **Logbook → ⋮ → Clean holds** (**Logbuch → ⋮ → Griffe putzen**).
The option remains available with an empty logbook. It offers board connection
when disconnected, identifies the connected controller, shows how many distinct
holds are left to clean and lights them in one color. Statistics and filters
keep their existing behavior; a logbook filter does not change the collection,
which always reads all of today's entries. Selection and deletion are in the
same overflow menu so the header still fits on narrow screens.

Closing the sheet leaves cleaning active. A persistent status button on board
screens reopens the controls. **Stop · keep collection** restores the last
known display; **Cleaned · reset collection** also marks the lit holds clean, so
only entries logged afterwards count. If the previous display is unknown after a
reconnect/restart or a raw relay write, finishing clears the LEDs. A failed
restore retains the collection and offers retry. Older API-2 controllers light
at most 127 holds per section, in one packet. **These holds are clean · next
section** marks only the confirmed section and lights the next; the final
confirmation restores the previous display. Unfinished sections stay in the
collection. Disconnect ends the active mode without marking anything.

## What is collected

- The holds of the climbs logged today in this phone's logbook (its secure
  database): sends and attempts, read when the sheet opens, on every
  (re)connection, on returning to the app, every 30 seconds while it is open
  and again right before lighting. Edited or deleted entries count as they are
  then. Sending or auto-sending a problem without logging it adds nothing.
- In a group, everyone cleans their own logged problems in turn, each with
  their own phone. There is no cross-phone aggregation or sync.
- Only entries that fit the connected board: its brand, and the active board's
  layout and size as a send checks them. Each climb's holds are worked out like
  a send of it: catalogue frames (all frames of a route), mirrored when logged
  mirrored on a mirrorable layout, the active size's placement-to-LED map (a
  climb none of whose holds that size maps does not count), on API 2 only
  positions its packets can address; MoonBoard through its own encoder for the
  climb's variant. A logbook-only climb missing from the catalogue uses the
  frames its send stored; an attempt of such a climb stores none and is
  skipped. This is not touch detection: a logged problem may include holds the
  climber did not actually use.
- The logbook does not record the physical controller, so two boards of the
  same brand, layout and size show the same entries. The cleaned marks are
  separate per physical controller/family/protocol.
- The local calendar date defines “today”, the logbook's own day key (the first
  ten characters of `climbed_at`). Midnight starts a fresh collection; an
  already active cleaning round can finish with its original holds.

**Cleaned** stores one mark per hashed controller in `board_cleaning`
preferences: the date, the local time the round started and the LED positions
that count as clean for entries logged up to then (also after a confirmed API-2
section). Entries logged later count in full. Marks of other days are dropped
on the next save; the per-day LED collection of earlier 0.2.4 builds is removed
on the first read. No logbook content, climb IDs or account data is stored or
logged, and cleaning creates no logbook or projection-history rows.

## Protocol and session boundaries

Aurora-family boards use single-channel blue with the connected encoder, preserving
API-2 power scaling and multi-packet framing. API-2 sections cap the number of
holds so every position stays nonzero at minimum brightness within the 18 W
budget; a large cyan union would otherwise quantize entirely to black. MoonBoard
uses its ASCII UART encoder with existing write pacing; cleaning marks each
physical hold below the hold, independent of above/both preview settings.

Quantum is unavailable: its existing adapter deliberately rejects unverified
multi-frame activation and requires scoped user/route ownership. This feature
must not bypass those guards to light an arbitrary day's union.

All local projection/clear/raw producers share the BLE write mutex and reject
writes during cleaning. The idle-disconnect timer is suppressed. Link changes
invalidate pending completions so an old write cannot clear another board's
collection. Other phones connected directly to a multi-client controller can
still overwrite its display; CruxCoach cannot lock those devices.

Starting requires the phone's relay sharing to be off and a direct
physical-board connection; a relay phone address does not identify a stable
physical board. Queue navigation is not discarded or logged by cleaning;
explicit normal sending resumes after exit.

## Validation

Focused tests cover: only logged entries count (sends and attempts), sent or
auto-sent problems add nothing, other days/brands/layouts/sizes do not count,
mirroring, route frames, catalogue fallback, API-2 address range, MoonBoard
positions and layout, edits and deletions at the next read, reads for an old
connection, “Cleaned” keeping only later entries across restarts and per
controller, entries imported during a round, midnight, API-2 single-packet
sections at the link's pacing, MoonBoard UART pacing, restoration and reset, the
relay lock and the sheet's texts and busy/empty controls. Full checks run in CI.

Physical-device acceptance before release:

1. Log a send and an attempt of two overlapping problems, one of them mirrored,
   and browse (auto-send) a third without logging it. Verify the count and the
   lit holds against the actual wall: the third problem's holds stay dark.
2. Open from the logbook, start, close the sheet and navigate. Ensure automatic
   sends and configured idle disconnect do not overwrite/end the cleaning mode.
3. Stop and verify the exact previous colors/positions; start again and mark
   cleaned, then log another problem and verify that only its holds count.
4. Delete a logged entry and reopen the sheet: its holds are gone. Restart the
   app on the same board, switch to a second controller and return; verify the
   cleaned mark per controller and the clear fallback when no previous display
   is known. Interrupt a write to check error/retry and collection retention.
5. Repeat on Aurora API-2/API-3 and MoonBoard standard/Mini; include more than
   127 holds on API 2. Verify readability at 320 dp/200% font size.
